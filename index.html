    How to play 🫧

    • 👆 Poke bubbles with any fingertip. Swipe fast through lots of them for combos.
    • 💖 Hearts fill your love meter. Big hearts with a bow burst into 3 mini hearts.
    • 🫶 Heart hands (touch thumbs together and index fingers together) when the meter is full: Love Blast pops everything!
    • 🌈 Rainbow = double points for 5 seconds. 🌙 Moon = dreamy slow-mo. ⭐ Stars are fast but worth the most.
    • ☁️ Grumpy clouds cost points and break your combo. Leave them alone!
    • ✌️ Peace sign on the results screen takes a selfie for your story card.
    • No camera? Tap or swipe with every finger. Tap the full heart for a Love Blast.

    Hand tracking runs entirely in your browser with Google's MediaPipe. No video or photos are uploaded.
